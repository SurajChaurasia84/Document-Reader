Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 6, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to PDF Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Offline-First Commitment: PDF Studio is designed to function primarily offline. Your document processing, including PDF viewing, editing, and scanning, happens directly on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifiers like your name, email address, or phone number. The app operates locally on your device to provide its core features:

  • Documents & Files: The app requires access to your device's storage to read, edit, and manage PDF and other document files. These files remain on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Camera Data: Access to your camera is used solely for the document scanning feature. Images captured are processed locally on your device to create PDFs.

Permissions

To provide a full-featured experience, PDF Studio requests the following permissions:

  • Storage/Photos/Media: To select, manage, and save documents.
  • Camera: To scan physical documents and convert them to PDF.
  • Internet Access: Required for displaying advertisements (via Unity Ads).

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Unity Ads: We use Unity Ads to support the development of this free application. Unity may collect data such as your IP address, device ID, and ad interaction data to serve relevant advertisements. Please refer to Unity's Privacy Policy for more details.

Data Security

We implement security measures to maintain the safety of your information. Since your documents are stored locally, their security depends on your device's overall security settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
